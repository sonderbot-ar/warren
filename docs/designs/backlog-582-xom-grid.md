# Backlog #582: score Exxon past 2026-08-03, now that its successor's filings are read

Issue: https://github.com/sonderbot-ar/warren/issues/582 (from the #573 review: analyst and product lead) · Branch `backlog-582-xom-grid` · Version v0.10.46 when merged.

Status: Done (v0.10.46, nov.25.2026; PR #589, merge `a5144f2`, after CI green and a gatekeeper GO).

## Problem

Since v0.10.44 (#573) Warren reads ExxonMobil's 10-Q and 10-K filings on its new CIK (ExxonMobil Holdings Corp, 0002115436) into the old CIK's history, so answers dated from 2026-08-03 see FY2026 Q2 and Q3. The evaluation did not follow: the grid (`grid._horizon`) and the harness's coverage guard (`harness.guard_coverage`) still cut Exxon at `history_ends` (2026-08-03). So:

- the grid file left out 8 Exxon dates (2026-02-15 and the seven post-cutoff dates) and shortened two (2025-05-15 to 3 quarters, 2025-11-15 to 1);
- the monthly forecasts (M9's origins, `ranges.origin_case`) take their horizon from the same guard: Exxon's ten 2026 origins are stored as failed ("coverage: XOM's filings here stop on 2026-08-03") and its 2025-04 to 2025-12 origins are cut to 3, 2 and 1 quarters, so its last scored quarter is FY2025Q4 while the other companies run into 2026;
- so Exxon's typical forecast errors (the past-events answer's usual miss and the track record's monthly section, both read from `baseline_errors`) and its grid record stop early, and the past-events notes say so (`beyond_history`, `history_cut` and `thin_evidence`'s `cut` with `successor_linked: true`), although the filings are now there.

## Plan

**Step 0. This doc.**

**Step 1. The grid and its guard use `coverage_ends`.**
- `grid._horizon` and `harness.guard_coverage` read `UniverseCompany.coverage_ends` (None for a linked company) instead of `history_ends`. The rule itself (the longest horizon up to 4 the guard accepts) is unchanged; for an unlinked company nothing changes.
- `coverage_gap` loses its `scored` flag and the linked-successor text it gave only under it: with the grid following the link there is no separate scored cut. The past-events call drops its three #573 branches (the "track record has not been re-run" words and `successor_linked` in the `beyond_history`, `history_cut` and `thin_evidence` params), which can no longer be reached.
- `grid_cases.csv` regenerated by the rule (`python -m domains.evaluation grid --write`): 176 cases (8 companies × 22 dates), Exxon on every date at 4 quarters; nothing else moves.
- Reading rules: fingerprint `4738db24de4b` logged under v15 (unscored calls return exactly what they did; no run reads a different figure).
- Tests: the grid's coverage-rule test now runs on an unlinked Exxon (`unlinked_xom`), and a new one checks the linked universe gives 176 cases, Exxon at 4 everywhere, each passing the guard; the harness's hard-error test runs on an unlinked Exxon and a new one checks the linked guard lets a 4-quarter horizon from 2026-10-15 through; the universe tests check there is no `scored` flag and an unlinked company is still cut with "missing"; the two #573 past-events tests become "a linked company (question or case) gets no coverage note, no cut flag and no cut in thin evidence".

Exit: full suite, `ruff check`, `ruff format --check` green.

**Step 2. The AI run for the 8 new Exxon dates.** Snapshot restored; `run-grid --ticker XOM --from 2026-01-01 --max-usd 10 --since 2026-11-03T00:00:00Z`, so the owner's $10 M10 cap counts everything spent since the grid run began ($5.70 so far). **Step 2b. Recorded: Exxon's origins and scored errors before and after.

**Step 3. A new grid batch, before and after.** Filings refreshed, then `backtest --cases-file domains/evaluation/grid_cases.csv --actuals-as-of 2026-11-24` (the last ended New York day, fixed before the run), `reproduce --batch`, `ranges test --batch`, `ranges reproduce`. M10's pre-registered result (`eval_2026-11-17_1`, 168 cases) stays as recorded; the new batch is a later grid batch under the same rule, and the app's track record reads it (`track_record.grid_batch`, the newest clean grid batch). Then `ranges test` builds a new range build (the stored `ranges_2026-11-17_1` is the "before"; the new build reads Exxon's new errors, so it is not expected to match it). Recorded below: counts, the headline table beside M10's, Exxon's own record (including its +36% revenue step from FY2026 Q1 to Q2, real in both filings), whether the other seven companies' grid figures move, and `analysis reproduce --all`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave the grid cut and keep the notes.** Honest but stale: the filings are in, and every month the gap in Exxon's record grows.
- **Re-run only Exxon in a separate batch.** The track record reads one batch, so Exxon would need its own reader path; one batch for all 176 cases keeps one reader and lets the before/after be read on the same rule.
- **Move GRID_END forward at the same time.** More post-cutoff dates for everyone, but a larger AI spend and a separate question; left for a later grid run.
- **Rewrite M10's recorded result.** No: that batch was pre-registered on 168 cases; it stays, and the new batch is reported beside it.

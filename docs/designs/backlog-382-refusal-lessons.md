# Backlog #382: a lesson per refusal group, starting with inconsistent data

Issue: https://github.com/sonderbot-ar/warren/issues/382 (from the #376 review) · Branch `backlog-382-refusal-lessons` · Version v0.9.72 on merge.

## Problem

Every run warning has a teaching lesson (`text.WARNING_LESSONS`, v0.9.60, #197): why it matters, what to check, and a published source or the Warren rule behind it. Refusals got codes and groups in v0.9.69 (#376: `coded.REFUSAL_GROUPS`, `REFUSAL_GROUP_ORDER`), but no lesson. The brief's refusal screen (`docs/product/brief.md`, "When an estimate can't be built") gives a title, one cause sentence and a button; two of its notes wait on this issue: the professor's point that a short equity risk premium window is too noisy (`erp_too_few_years`), and "a lesson per refusal group is asked for in #382" (`no_base_value`).

The issue asks for: a lesson per group, starting with `inconsistent_data` (the accounting identities a filing should satisfy: no negative balance, securities not above current assets), each saying what the rule is, why a real company can break it and what the student can do; a test that every group has one; and for `erp_too_few_years` the standard error a 10-year window would have, about 6 points, checked against Damodaran's ERP paper.

## Plan

- **Step 0.** This design doc. No build plan change (a backlog item).

- **Step 1. The lessons, as data beside the warning lessons.** In `domains/analysis/text.py`, reusing `Lesson` (its `group` is now a refusal group for these):
  - `REFUSAL_GROUP_LESSONS`: one lesson per group, keys in `REFUSAL_GROUP_ORDER`. `later_date` (point in time: a forecast needs years of history published before its date), `earlier_date` (only days ended in New York with prices downloaded), `company_data` (a figure missing or zero in the filings; gaps are common), `inconsistent_data` (no negative balance; short-term securities are part of current assets; the filing almost always put the wrong number under the line; open the report), `no_value` (the Gordon terminal value is finite only while growth stays below the cost of capital), `setup` (Warren's own data isn't loaded; not the student's doing).
  - `REFUSAL_LESSONS`: a code's own lesson where the group's is too general. First: `balance_item_negative` and `securities_exceed_current_assets` (the two `inconsistent_data` codes) and `erp_too_few_years`, whose params are `min_years` (`erp.MIN_YEARS`, 10), `sd` (0.2, a fixed constant: the annual standard deviation of stock returns as Damodaran rounds it, standing in for the premium's own), `se` (`sd / √min_years` rounded to 0.06, shown "about 6 percentage points") and `two_se` (0.13, shown "13 points higher or lower", the rough 95% range Damodaran draws). *As built after review; the first draft showed one standard error as "6.32 points either way".* The stance taught: even Warren's 10-year minimum leaves a standard error close to the size of the premium itself; it is a floor, not a comfortable window.
  - `refusal_lesson(code)`: the code's own lesson, else its group's. Every code in `ENGINE_REFUSAL_CODES` gets one.
  - Rules carried over from the warning lessons: numbers only through params (a changed `MIN_YEARS` changes the error shown), no code names in the English, a published `reference` or a `warren_rule` (or both) on every lesson. One difference: up to three sentences, since a refusal lesson says the rule, why a real company or date hits it, and what to do next (a warning's lesson has two).
  - Source checked: Damodaran, *Equity Risk Premiums (ERP): Determinants, Estimation and Implications, The 2022 Edition* (updated March 23, 2022), Table 2 "Standard Errors in Historical Risk Premiums": 10 years, 20%/√10 = 6.32%. The 2024 edition the issue names (Table 5) isn't on Damodaran's site under a guessable file name; the 2022 edition was read, so it is the one cited. The table is the same formula, 20%/√n, so the issue's "about 6 points" is confirmed. The current-assets rule is ARB No. 43, chapter 3A, paragraph 4 (now FASB ASC 210-10-45-1); assets and liabilities as amounts held or owed are FASB Concepts Statement No. 8, chapter 4 (December 2021, which replaced Concepts Statement No. 6; the first draft cited CON 6).
  Tests (`tests/analysis/test_refusal_lessons.py`): every group has a lesson in screen order; code lessons are real codes in their own group; both `inconsistent_data` codes and `erp_too_few_years` have one; `refusal_lesson` covers every coded refusal; the plain-English rules above; the ERP numbers come from `erp.MIN_YEARS` and give 6.32 at 10 years; the setup lesson says it doesn't depend on the student.
  Exit: tests pass; full suite and ruff clean.

No engine value, run, note, code or refusal text changes. `text.py` is not imported by `math_engine` or `facts`, so the reading rules' fingerprint (v0.9.67) does not move; no snapshot is needed and no stored run is touched.

## Design review (nov.14.2026)

Explainer, analyst, professor and the product lead, in parallel, on the first draft.

- **Analyst and professor, BLOCKING, accepted.** The `inconsistent_data` lessons said "the filing has almost always put the wrong number under that line". The engine's own refusal says "a tag picks up the wrong measure" (`math_engine.py`, `securities_exceed_current_assets`), and on Phase 1 companies the likely cause is Warren's own concept-map fallbacks: `cash` can fall back to the tag that includes restricted cash (`concept_map.csv`, PG FY2020+, CAT FY2016–20), and `marketable_securities_current` to the generic `MarketableSecurities`, which can be current plus non-current. A student who opens the 10-K would find a correct figure. The three lessons now say the figure Warren read most likely belongs to a different measure, through the line Warren chose or the way the figure was tagged, with no "almost always" (no frequency evidence); a test pins it.
- **Analyst, notes, accepted.** The group lesson now names cash with securities (the second check, `with_cash`); the securities lesson names an older balance line set against a newer current-assets total (`balance_carried_forward`); the ERP lesson says the refusal gives the first date with enough years (`first_as_of`, v0.9.71).
- **Professor, notes, accepted.** CON 8 chapter 4 in place of CON 6 (replaced in 2021). The `no_value` lesson says the sum of growing cash flows has a value only while the cost of capital is above zero and growth stays below it (the first draft's "finite" was loose and left out two of `no_base_value`'s three reasons, `dcf.py`). "6.32 either way" read as a hard limit with false precision: now "a standard error of about 6 points … 13 points higher or lower". The 20% stand-in is said in a comment.
- **Product lead, BLOCKING, accepted.** For `no_value` the brief's button "Ver por qué" already opens *Concepto*; a second "¿Por qué?" link would add a second door. Product impact now says: for `no_value` the button opens this lesson inside *Concepto*, and there is no separate link. Notes taken: no blame on the filing (as above, passive "was tagged"); the `no_value` lesson covers all three reasons; #344 (a revision mark for reworded lessons) covers these lessons too.
- **Explainer.** Gaps answered in the plan: `sd` is a fixed constant, its source and the stance taught; the 2022 edition confirms the 2024 issue's figure; `MIN_YEARS` is in `domains/market/erp.py`; the "plain English" rules are checked by `test_lessons_are_plain_short_sentences_with_their_params` (no backticks or underscores, no digit outside params, up to three sentences). The app's catalogue test is front-end work, not this merge.

## Alternatives considered

- **Only group lessons.** The issue's own evidence is a code lesson (`erp_too_few_years`), and `inconsistent_data`'s two codes break different rules; one paragraph for both would teach neither well. Codes without their own lesson fall back to the group's.
- **A lesson for every code now.** 25 codes, most of them a missing figure whose group lesson already says what to do. Left for the screens, which will show which ones students meet (brief: "the rest follow the same pattern, written with the screens").
- **A new `RefusalLesson` class.** Same fields as `Lesson`; one shape lets the app render both with one component.
- **Lessons in `coded.py`.** `coded.py` holds codes and their closed value sets; words live in `text.py` (Decision 11, guardrail 5), as the warning lessons do.

Added, nothing renamed or removed: `text.REFUSAL_GROUP_LESSONS` (one `Lesson` per group in `REFUSAL_GROUP_ORDER`), `text.REFUSAL_LESSONS` (`balance_item_negative`, `securities_exceed_current_assets`, `erp_too_few_years`; params on the last: `min_years` int, `sd` and `se` fractions) and `text.refusal_lesson(code)`. The app writes its own Spanish from the same params, as for `WARNING_LESSONS`, and its catalogue test can now require a lesson key per group and per entry of `REFUSAL_LESSONS`. Suggested place on the screen: a "¿Por qué?" link under the cause sentence that opens the lesson, so the screen keeps its three parts; for `no_value`, whose button "Ver por qué" already opens *Concepto*, the lesson goes inside *Concepto* and there is no separate link. The English never says the filing or the company is wrong: a figure Warren read "most likely belongs to a different measure". A reworded lesson isn't caught by the app's catalogue test today; #344 (the revision mark) covers these lessons too. The brief's `erp_too_few_years` and `no_base_value` notes can point here.

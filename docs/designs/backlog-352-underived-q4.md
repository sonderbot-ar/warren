# Backlog #352: say when a newer report is in but its quarter wasn't read, and the late-filing notice

Issue: https://github.com/sonderbot-ar/warren/issues/352 · Branch `backlog-352-underived-q4` · Status: In review (nov.13.2026)

## Problem

The review of backlog #349 (PR #351, v0.9.62) left three follow-ups on the stale warning.

1. **The wrong advice when the 10-K is in.** A run starts from the newest quarter whose revenue Warren has (`math_engine._last_actual_quarter`). A fourth quarter exists only as a derivation, year minus the first three quarters (`domains/fundamentals/facts.py`). If the 10-K is loaded but that derivation fails (the year's revenue is missing, or sits under a tag the concept map doesn't read), the newest quarter stays Q3. Then two things go wrong:
   - Past 161 days, `fundamentals_stale` tells the student to "check that the newest filings are ingested". They are. The fault is that the quarter could not be read or derived.
   - Before 161 days, nothing is said at all, although the run starts one report behind what is public. That silent case is the more common one: a 10-K lands about 60 days after the year ends, so for the next 100 days every run quietly ignores it.
   The same can happen after a 10-Q whose revenue line isn't read: the run starts from the quarter before, and says nothing until 135 days.
2. **The late-filing notice.** Exchange Act Rule 12b-25 lets a company file Form NT 10-K or NT 10-Q, by the next business day after the deadline, and get 15 or 5 more calendar days; a report filed within them counts as on time (17 CFR 240.12b-25(b)). The lesson says "or the company filed late", which misnames that case.
3. **A clearer failure for the rule test** (gatekeeper, PR #351). `test_the_stale_rule_names_each_line_with_its_own_parts` reads the rule with a regex; a wording edit gives an empty list and a failure that doesn't say why.

## Plan

- **Step 0.** This design doc. No build plan change (backlog item).
- **Step 1. A note when a newer report is in but its quarter wasn't read.** In `_last_actual_quarter` (`domains/analysis/math_engine.py`), after picking the newest revenue quarter, look for any *duration* figure (a flow over a period, `period_start` set) of any item the run loads (`math_engine.ITEMS`: revenue, costs, income, cash flow lines; all of them, since any one dated later shows a newer report is in), quarterly or annual, in the history `load_history` already read as of the run's date, that ends after that quarter. Balances (instants) are left out because the cover page's share count is dated after the quarter by design. If one exists, a newer report is loaded:
  - New run-note code `newer_report_not_read`, params `last_quarter_end`, `newer_period_end` (the latest such end), `newer_annual` (the figure covers more than 300 days, a fiscal year), `newer_accession_no` (the filing that gave it, so the app can link it; a reported figure is preferred to a derived one) and `days` (as-of minus `last_quarter_end`). English: "The newest quarter with revenue ended {last}, but figures from the annual report for the year (or: a report for the period) ended {newer} are loaded: that period's revenue could not be read or derived, so the run starts at least one report behind (check that filing's revenue line)."
  - It fires whatever the age. The stale line then keys on the newer period instead of the newest revenue quarter (after a year end the next report is a 10-Q, 135 days), so `fundamentals_stale` no longer gives the wrong advice for the unread report, but still fires if a report after it is missing too (analyst: two faults at once). A run without a newer duration figure is unchanged.
  - `coded.RUN_NOTE_CODES` gains the code; `text.WARNING_LESSONS` gains its lesson (group `data`, no params). Its `why`, which the app translates: "A company files no report for its fourth quarter, so Warren works that quarter's revenue out as the year's revenue from the annual report minus the first three quarters, and reads every other quarter from its own report. Here a newer report is loaded but that revenue could not be read or worked out, so the run starts from an earlier quarter and is at least one report behind: check the revenue line in the report this warning names." Its Warren rule: "Warren's own check (math_engine._last_actual_quarter): a figure for a period that ends after the newest revenue quarter means a newer report is loaded; balances are not counted, since the cover page's share count is dated after the quarter").
  - Tests (`tests/analysis/test_build_warning_codes.py`, on Test Corp from `tests/analysis/conftest.py`, as of 2020-06-30 unless stated): the #349 fixture `without_fy2019_revenue` (FY2019 10-K in, its full-year revenue row dropped) now gives `newer_report_not_read` at 2020-03-09 (161 days after Q3, before the old stale line) and at 2020-03-10 (162 days), with every param and the text, and no `fundamentals_stale` (`test_a_10k_without_a_derivable_fourth_quarter_says_the_newer_report_was_not_read`); the accession is the one the year's figures came from; the same fixture at 2020-05-15 (136 days after the year end, no Q1 10-Q) gives both notes and at 2020-05-14 only the new one; a new fixture `without_q1_2020_revenue` (the Q1 2020 10-Q in, its revenue dropped) gives the new note with `newer_annual` false; the unedited fixture `company` (whose cover share count is dated after its quarter) gives neither; `without_q1_2020` (the 10-Q not filed) gives `fundamentals_stale` only. The lesson tests check the new lesson teaches the subtraction (professor).
  - Exit: tests pass. Checked by hand: dropping the instant filter fails 26 tests (the cover count fires everywhere); keying the stale line on the old quarter again fails 4.
- **Step 2. The late-filing notice in the lesson.** `math_engine` gains `NT_EXTENSION_10Q_DAYS = 5` and `NT_EXTENSION_10K_DAYS = 15` beside the deadlines, documented as law (Rule 12b-25(b)) that the lines do **not** add. The `fundamentals_stale` lesson's first sentence is unchanged; its second becomes "Warren may be missing a filing, the company filed late, or it filed a late-filing notice by the next business day, which keeps the report on time if it arrives within {nt_10q} more calendar days for a quarterly report or {nt_10k} for an annual one: check the company's list of filings." (professor: the extension holds only if the report arrives within it). Params gain `nt_10q` and `nt_10k`; the reference gains "Exchange Act Rule 12b-25(b) (Form NT 10-K and NT 10-Q, the late-filing notice)"; the Warren rule adds "the late-filing notice's 5 and 15 extra days are not added to either line, so a run can warn about a report that is still on time" (analyst: 92 + 40 + 5 = 137 is past 135). Tests: `THRESHOLDS` gains both; the lesson stays two sentences.
  - Exit: lesson tests pass.
- **Step 3. The rule test fails clearly.** Assert the regex finds exactly two clauses, with a message quoting the rule, before comparing them. Exit: replacing "plus the" with "and the" in the rule fails with that message (checked by hand, not committed).

## Alternatives considered

- **A param on `fundamentals_stale` (`annual_report_ingested`)**, the issue's first option. Rejected: the warning would still fire only after 161 days, so the common case (a run in the 100 days after the 10-K) would stay silent, and the lesson the student reads would still say "check the filings are ingested".
- **Keep both notes when both apply.** Rejected: two warnings for one fault, one of them with the wrong fix.
- **Only the 10-K case** (last quarter Q3, a newer annual figure). Rejected: the same check costs nothing for a 10-Q whose revenue wasn't read, and the advice is the same.
- **Add the notice's extra days to the lines.** Rejected: a company that files the notice is still late against its normal deadline, and Warren does not read NT filings; the lesson names the case instead.

## Step 4 results (nov.13.2026)

3,216 company-dates (8 companies × 402 Fridays): the new note fires on **none**. The analyst ran the same check independently and got the same count. So no real run today has an unread newer report, and no false alarm: items filed only yearly (`pretax_income_domestic`, `interest_paid`) end on the same day as a derived fourth quarter (`facts.py`, `_derive` keeps the year's end), and no flow figure in the database ends after its filing date (analyst, a query on `financial_facts`). `fundamentals_stale` would fire on the same 13 company-dates as on `main` (all Exxon, after its filings stop, 2026-08-03). The fixture is the only true case; the note is a guard for a tag change or a missing line in a future filing, which is how the #349 case was first found.

## Known limits

- A fiscal-year change is filed as a 10-KT, which the loader doesn't read (`domains/fundamentals/ingest.py`, `FORMS`), so a transition period looks like no report and falls back to the stale line (analyst). No company in the universe has changed its fiscal year since 2016.
- The check sees only the items Warren reads (`math_engine.ITEMS`). A newer report from which Warren reads nothing at all looks like no report, and falls back to the stale line as today.
- Changing the stale advice does not change stored runs' note text; a stored run keeps the note it was made with.

**Point in time.** The check reads only the history the run already loaded as of its date. **LLMs.** None. **Decision 11.** No source, company or currency change. **Numbers.** No value, assumption or output changes; only which note a run carries.

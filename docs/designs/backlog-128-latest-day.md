# Backlog #128: runs default to the latest ended day; a past date shows what happened next

Issue: https://github.com/sonderbot-ar/warren/issues/128 · Branch `backlog-128-latest-day` · Version v0.9.7 when merged.

## Problem

Asked by the owner on nov.02.2026; the front-end team names it as the second thing F2's first screen needs (brief questions 3 and 5, `docs/product/brief.md` §2 "Point in time").

1. Every command that values or reads a company needs `--as-of` (`domains/orchestration/__main__.py:198`, `domains/analysis/__main__.py:253` and `:288`). A user who wants "now" has to type a date, and if they type today's date the run is refused, because the day has not ended in New York (`nodes.check_closed`, backlog #112).
2. A forecast as of a past date forecasts quarters that are already reported, but the pipeline report never shows those actuals. A reader sees a forecast without the one thing that makes a past date worth asking about: how far off it was.

Item 2 of the issue (a completed period returns its reported figures) moved to #140 and shipped in v0.9.6. This item keeps items 1 and 3.

## What changes

- **The default date.** "Today" means the last day that has ended in New York: `shared_kernel.calendar.last_ended_day(now)` (`now` must carry a time zone), the newest date whose `known_by` is at or before `now`. At 23:59 New York time on nov.03 that is nov.02; at 00:00 on nov.04 it is nov.03. Three user-facing commands use it when `--as-of` is left out and say which day they chose: `python main.py run` (from `deps.now()`), `python -m domains.analysis dcf` (with `--ticker` or `--all`; `--as-of` still repeats) and `python -m domains.analysis reported` (with `--json`, the note goes to standard error so the JSON is unchanged; `as_of` is already in it). `--as-of` given on purpose keeps today's behaviour exactly: that is the issue's "past-date forecast as an explicit option". The batch commands (`evaluation run`, `backtest`, `ranges`) are unchanged: they always name their dates.
- **Stale filings are said, not hidden** (analyst). The pipeline downloads filings again when its stored download is older than the as-of day (`nodes.load_fundamentals`). `dcf` and `reported` read what is stored. When they chose the date themselves, they print the date of the company's last EDGAR download, and a warning when it is older than the end of the as-of day: "filings after <day> may be missing: run `python -m domains.fundamentals ingest --ticker T`". Prices already refuse a stale download (`market.store.require_coverage`), so `dcf` without `--as-of` is refused after each New York midnight until prices are loaded again; its message names the fix (test in Step 2).
- **Forecast against what was reported.** A new call, `domains.evaluation.hindsight.against_actuals(conn, run_id, actuals_on)`. For each forecast quarter of the stored run (the run's own quarterly outputs, Q1–Q8, in order; `horizon` 1–8), for revenue and EBIT:
  - `forecast` is the run's output. `actual` is the value as first reported in filings public by `actuals_on` (M3's rule, `get_facts(..., mode="first_reported")`). EBIT is on the run's own definition: the EBIT chain alternative recorded on the run's `history:ebit` row for the same quarter a year earlier (`harness._alternative`, as the harness does); for quarters 5–8, whose year-earlier quarter was itself forecast, the run's newest `history:ebit` row (found on the real run: without it every quarter past the first year came back `no_definition`).
  - `signed_miss` is the second value of `harness.score`: (forecast − actual) ÷ |actual| for revenue, (forecast − actual) ÷ |actual revenue| for EBIT, so EBIT's is margin points ÷ 100. `miss` is its absolute value. `miss_unit` says which: `fraction_of_actual` or `fraction_of_actual_revenue`. Positive means the forecast was too high.
  - Gaps are coded, never raised, each `{code, params, text}` as in #140; `actual`, `miss` and `signed_miss` are None with a code: `not_reported` (the item is not in a 10-Q or 10-K public by `actuals_on`; params `period`, `item`, `actuals_on`; for EBIT, also when revenue is reported and no EBIT definition is), `other_ebit_definition` (EBIT is reported, but only on another chain alternative; params `period`, `definition`, `reported_as`), `no_definition` (the run has no `history:ebit` row at all, so neither the year-earlier row nor the newest one gives a definition; params `period`), `zero_base` (the scale is zero; params `period`).
  - **A change of basis is not a miss** (analyst). The harness's `comparative` gives, from the filing that reported the actual, the same quarter a year earlier. When that differs from the run's own `history:` row for that quarter by more than 0.5% (`BASIS_TOLERANCE`), the filing has recast the prior year (a disposal moved to discontinued operations, as at JNJ in its Q3 2023 10-Q, or a segment change), so the actual is shown but `miss` and `signed_miss` are None with `restated_basis` (params `period`, `prior_period`, `run_value`, `filing_value`). For quarters 5–8 the year-earlier figure the run's basis implies is that quarter's own actual; once an item's basis has changed, every later quarter of that item is flagged too (the forecast still sits on the old basis). When the comparative can't be built (a mixed-vintage derived quarter), nothing is flagged.
  - Each item also carries `unit` (the fact's unit, e.g. `USD`; values raw, not in millions), `filings[]` (the #140 `Filing` shape: accession, form, filing date, EDGAR folder link; for a derived quarter, the filings it was derived from), `derived`, and the quarter carries `fiscal_year`, `fiscal_period`, and when reported `period_end` and `days` (the quarter's length, so a 14-week quarter is visible). A quarter not reported has no period end: the engine doesn't know it until it is filed (52/53-week years), and guessing one would be a number nobody reported.
- **In the pipeline report.** The report gets a section "6. Forecast against what was reported" (the trace becomes section 7) whenever the pipeline's state has `actuals_on`. `actuals_on` is a new optional field of the checkpointed pipeline state (no new column), set by `write_report` to the earlier of `last_ended_day(deps.now())` and `last_ended_day(<when the filings the pipeline used were downloaded>)`, from the pipeline's ingest run (professor and analyst: a date later than the download would claim filings the database never saw). Every filing public by `actuals_on` was therefore in the download, so a later ingest can only add filings dated after it, and the section renders to the same text every time: `reproduce` keeps comparing it. The section explains "as first reported" in one line (the value in the first 10-Q or 10-K that reported it, not a later restatement), gives revenue misses in % and EBIT misses in margin points, and notes that M3 measured the first four quarters only. When no forecast quarter was reported by `actuals_on` (every run as of the latest day), the section is one line saying so. Pipelines written before this version have no `actuals_on`: their reports render as before, without the section, so they still reproduce.

**Point in time.** Nothing that feeds the forecast changes: the run, the news, the shocks and the simulation read only what was public by `as_of`, and the report's sections 1–5 are untouched. Section 6 is hindsight by design, labelled as such, read only for the report, and never written back into a run. It reads filings public by `actuals_on`, so it is point-in-time on its own date. This is the same split the evaluation harness has made since M3.

## Steps

- **Step 0.** This design doc; the build plan is unchanged (a backlog item).
- **Step 1.** `last_ended_day` in `shared_kernel/calendar.py`. Tests (`tests/shared_kernel/test_calendar.py`): one second before New York midnight gives the day before, midnight the day that just ended, in summer and winter; the same at both 2026 clock changes; `known_by(day) <= now < known_by(day + 1)` for a sweep of instants across a year; a naive datetime is refused. Exit: green.
- **Step 2.** Defaults in the three commands, the download date and the stale warning. Tests: `run` without `--as-of` (world clock 2026-11-01 20:00 UTC) starts `TST_2026-10-31_p1` and says it chose the day (the Test Corp market data stops in 2020, so the run then fails on prices: the test checks the day, not the run); with `--as-of` unchanged; `reported` and `dcf` without `--as-of` use `last_ended_day` of a patched clock, print the download date and the warning when the download is older; `reported --json` keeps standard output pure JSON; `dcf` without a date is refused on stale prices with a message naming the fix. Exit: green; every existing CLI test unchanged.
- **Step 3.** `domains/evaluation/hindsight.py`. Tests (`tests/evaluation/test_hindsight.py`, Test Corp from `tests/analysis/conftest.py`, numbers known by construction): a run as of 2019-06-30 against actuals on 2020-06-30 has FY2019Q2 to FY2020Q1 reported (FY2019Q4 derived, with its filings) and the rest `not_reported`; each `signed_miss` equals the M3 harness's baseline `signed_error` for the same case and quarter; actuals on the day before the Q1 2020 10-Q leave that quarter unreported; an edited fixture whose operating income is missing in one quarter gives `other_ebit_definition` (pretax + interest is there) and raises nothing; an edited fixture that recasts a prior-year comparative by 5% gives `restated_basis` with the actual shown and no miss, and 0.1% does not; `to_dict` round-trips through `json`. Exit: green.
- **Step 4.** The report section and `actuals_on`. Tests (`tests/orchestration/test_graph.py`, the Test Corp world): a new pipeline's report has section 6 with the one line "no forecast quarter in a 10-Q or 10-K by <day>" and section 7 is the trace; it renders the same twice and `reproduce` passes; `actuals_on` is capped by the download date when the download is older than the clock; a state without `actuals_on` renders without section 6 and with the trace as 6 (an old pipeline); section 6 on a state whose run is as of 2019-06-30 shows the reported quarters, the derived Q4 and misses in % and points. Exit: green; ruff clean.
- **Step 5.** Real data, on the restored snapshot: `against_actuals` on the stored baseline runs the M8 batch scored for MSFT 2022-06-02 and the other cases, with `actuals_on` = the batch's own `actuals_as_of` (from its notes), gives a `signed_miss` equal to the batch's baseline `signed_error` on every scored quarter, except any marked `restated_basis`, which are listed; `reproduce` passes on the stored M7 pipelines (no section 6, since they predate `actuals_on`). Written below.

**Exit criterion:** all tests pass with the existing suite; Step 5's misses equal the backtest's; `docs/team-sync.md` names the default and the new call.

## Alternatives considered

- *Default to the calendar's today.* Rejected: the engine refuses a day still in progress (#112), so the default would fail every time before midnight New York.
- *Render section 6 with the actuals known when the report is rendered.* Rejected: the report would change as new 10-Qs land and `reproduce` would report a mismatch on a correct pipeline. Storing `actuals_on` keeps the report reproducible; a fresh look is a new pipeline or, later, a `report --actuals-on` option (follow-up if the front-end asks).
- *Put the comparison in the `dcf` report too.* Deferred: the `dcf` report has no stored state to hold `actuals_on`, so it would change over time. The app gets the same numbers from `against_actuals` on any run.
- *A separate `backtest` mode flag instead of an explicit `--as-of`.* Rejected: the issue gives `--as-of` on purpose as the example, and a second flag that means "I typed a date" adds nothing.

## Product impact

Serves brief §2 "Point in time" (the default is the latest ended day; a past date shows what happened afterwards) and question 3 (MSFT's value today): Q3's note "the default to the latest date is #128 item 1" is removed at merge. Q5's forecast now has its misses on past dates; its range stays #144 and a benchmark beside the miss (Q6) stays #145. F2's first screen can call any of the three commands, or the library, without asking the student for a date.

Adds: `shared_kernel.calendar.last_ended_day(now)`; `domains.evaluation.hindsight.against_actuals(conn, run_id, actuals_on)` and its `to_dict`; the pipeline state field `actuals_on`; report section 6. Fields of `to_dict` (values raw and unrounded, in the item's `unit`; misses are fractions):

- top: `run_id`, `ticker`, `as_of`, `actuals_on`, `quarters[]`;
- a quarter: `period` (the run's label, e.g. `FY2019Q3`), `fiscal_year`, `fiscal_period` (`Q1`–`Q4`, the company's own fiscal labels), `horizon` (1–8), `period_end` and `days` (None until reported), `items[]`;
- an item: `name` (`revenue` or `ebit`), `unit`, `forecast`, `actual`, `miss`, `signed_miss`, `miss_unit` (`fraction_of_actual` or `fraction_of_actual_revenue`), `derived`, `filings[]` (each `{accession_no, form, filed_at, url}`, the #140 shape), `missing` (a coded or None);
- a coded: `code`, `params`, `text` (English fallback). Codes: `not_reported` (same meaning as #140's: not in a filing public by the date), `other_ebit_definition`, `no_definition`, `zero_base`, `restated_basis`.

Renamed or removed: on new reports only, the pipeline report's trace moves from section 6 to 7. The first-reported actual only (no latest value or `changed` flag): scoring uses the first-reported value; the latest is #140's `reported` call on the same period.

## Design review (nov.03.2026)

Explainer, analyst, professor and the front-end `product-lead` (on *Product impact*), in parallel. Analyst NO-GO on the first draft (3 blockers); professor 1 blocker; explainer 5; product lead 3. All accepted except where noted; the doc above is the revised design.

- **Analyst.** (1) `actuals_on` at the clock's day could claim filings the database never downloaded (a past run on an old download): now capped by the download date, with a test. (2) The default date made stale filings silent for `dcf` and `reported`: they now print the download date and warn; the price refusal after midnight is tested. (3) A recast prior year (JNJ's Kenvue in its Q3 2023 10-Q) would show as a 15–20% miss: now `restated_basis`, actual shown, no miss. Ideas taken: `days` on each quarter; "not yet in a 10-Q or 10-K" wording; the injected clock. Idea rejected: an expected period end for unreported quarters (a guess the filings haven't made; `fiscal_year` and `fiscal_period` given instead). Idea left: a note when a derived Q4 mixes bases in first-reported mode (its comparative then can't be built, so `restated_basis` can't fire): backlog.
- **Professor.** (1) A stored date doesn't pin the data (as analyst 1): same fix; the reasoning is in "In the pipeline report". Suggestions taken: item-level `not_reported` for EBIT; `miss_unit`; a one-line "as first reported" explanation; horizons 5–8 noted as beyond what M3 measured. Idea left: the naive benchmark beside each miss (brief Q6, #145).
- **Explainer.** (1) Gap classification: four codes, defined above. (2) Which `score` value is the miss: the signed error, defined above; zero scale is `zero_base`. (3) The EBIT definition (prior-year `history:ebit` row) and the horizon (the run's quarterly outputs): stated. (4) Step 5's `actuals_on` and runs: the batch's own. (5) `dcf --all` takes the default too: stated. Ideas taken: the injected clock; `now` must carry a time zone; derived filings through #140's `Filing` and its accession lookup; `actuals_on` lives in the checkpointed state. Idea left: `fallbacks` and the harness default to the machine's date (backlog).
- **Product lead.** (1) Units: stated (raw, fractions, `miss_unit`). (2) `miss` defined, gaps None with a code. (3) Code params listed; `fiscal_year` and `fiscal_period` added; `period_end` stays None until reported (see analyst, rejected idea). Ideas taken: none of the shape changes to `reported --json` (the chosen date goes to standard error; `as_of` is already in the JSON). Ideas left: first-reported and latest with `changed` (the `reported` call has it); ranges (#144) and a benchmark (#145).

## Results (nov.03.2026)

On the snapshot of nov.03 (`warren-20261103T070542Z`, restored from its split parts).

- **Same numbers as the backtest.** `against_actuals` on every baseline run of the three stored backtest batches, each with its own `actuals_as_of`: of 2,016 baseline rows (40 had no actual yet when the batch ran, and still have none at its date), 1,872 give a `signed_miss` equal to the batch's `signed_error` (to 1e-12) and 104 are marked `restated_basis`; none differs. On the M8 batch (`eval_2026-11-02_1`, 208 rows): 192 equal, 16 marked.
- **What the basis check finds.** Every mark is a real change of basis: Johnson & Johnson from its Q3 2023 10-Q (Kenvue moved to discontinued operations; the run had FY2022Q4 revenue at 23.7 billion, the filing 19.9 billion, and the backtest scored that as a 59% miss), Coca-Cola in 2019 (the bottler in Africa moved back from held-for-sale, so 2018 quarters grew about 5%), Exxon in 2019 (the comparative uses total revenues and other income, the run sales and operating revenue: another line, about 3%), and one Procter & Gamble derived fourth quarter (EBIT 3%). The backtest counts all of these as forecast error; that is a finding for the harness, filed as a backlog issue, not changed here.
- **Old pipelines render as before.** The sections a report compares (`report.render`) on MSFT 2022-06-02, WMT 2025-08-21, AAPL 2026-10-15 and JNJ 2023-05-15 hash the same under the old and the new code (no `actuals_on`, no section 6). The snapshot holds no report files, so `main.py reproduce` was not run on them; the render check is what it compares.
- **A real section 6.** MSFT as of 2022-06-02 against filings to 2026-11-02: revenue misses +2.4% (fiscal Q4 2022, derived) to +12.8% (Q2 2023), EBIT −0.88 to +7.29 margin points, every quarter with its 10-Q or 10-K. JNJ as of 2023-05-15: Q2 2023 −1.1% revenue, then every later quarter marked as a change of basis instead of the 16–25% "misses" the bare numbers would show.

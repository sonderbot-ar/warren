# Backlog #430: the current portion merge, paired before it is placed, and a note when rounding decides

Issue: https://github.com/sonderbot-ar/warren/issues/430 (from the PR #429 gatekeeper and analyst reviews of `docs/designs/backlog-427-current-merge-followups.md`) · Branch `backlog-430-current-merge-placing`.

## Background

Since v0.9.81 (#421) the current portion of long-term debt is filed under two map items, `debt_current` (`LongTermDebtCurrent`) and `debt_current_with_leases` (`LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligationsCurrent`). `facts.get_facts` reads both with `debt_noncurrent` and merges them per balance date into one `debt_current` (`_merge_current`, choosing with `current_on_noncurrent_basis`). Since v0.9.82 (#427) the pairing is on the date alone, the merged item keeps one fact per fiscal label in annual and quarterly mode, and a lease-inclusive figure below a plain figure that looks rounded is taken when it lies within half the plain figure's rounding unit (`_within_rounding`).

## Problem

1. **Placed before paired.** In annual and quarterly mode `get_facts` asks `_get_facts` for the three items *already placed*: annual keeps only `FY`-labelled facts, quarterly keeps one fact per (item, fiscal label), the later date winning (`_one_per_period`). Only then does `_merge_current` pair them by date. So if one filing placed a year-end lease-inclusive figure under `Q3`, or left it unplaced, the annual read drops it before it meets the plain figure at the same date (the plain figure is read alone); the quarterly read is worse, since a year end mislabelled `Q3` displaces the real `Q3` lease-inclusive figure (the later date wins the label) and the September plain figure is read alone. The #427 guard test only shifted `fiscal_year`, which both reads keep.
2. **No `get_facts`-level test of one-per-label.** #427's test calls `_merge_current` directly; nothing pins that `get_facts` passes the frequency down.
3. **No note when the rounding margin decides.** When `_within_rounding` picks a lease-inclusive figure *below* the plain one, nothing in a run says so; #412 and #415 have run notes. Raised by the professor at #427's design review and by the gatekeeper at its PR review.
4. **Watch the 10^7 floor.** 77 of 610 current-portion facts look rounded to 10^7 or coarser. At 10^7 the margin is 5M. The note of item 3 is how a future filing that trips the margin becomes visible; nothing else changes.

**On today's data nothing changes:** across every filing, no date has the two current tags under different fiscal labels or periods, and no lease-inclusive figure sits below a plain one (#427's survey and analyst check). This is robustness: the merged item behaves like a single map item by construction, and the one judgement call the reads make on rounding is shown in the run's notes when it decides the balance sheet the run values from (not when it decides the earlier year ends the cost of debt averages over; there the effect is at most half a unit, 5M at the 10^7 floor; analyst).

## Plan

- **Step 0.** This design doc.

- **Step 1. Pair every filed date, then place.**
  - `facts.get_facts`: in annual and quarterly mode, the three items of `CURRENT_ITEMS` (`debt_current`, `debt_current_with_leases`, `debt_noncurrent`) are read *as filed* (`_get_facts(..., "reported")`: one fact per item per date, same mode, same point-in-time query), paired by date with `_merge_current` called with frequency `"reported"` (any other frequency would dedupe per label first and bring the bug back), and only then placed by `_placed`: annual keeps the `FY`-labelled facts, quarterly relabels a year end's `FY` as `Q4` (as `_flow_years` does for every balance item), and both keep one fact per label, the later date winning (`_one_per_period`). Every other item, and `debt_noncurrent` or `debt_current_with_leases` when asked by name, comes from the usual placed read, unchanged. As for every balance item in `_flow_years` today, a quarterly read that holds an `FY` and a `Q4` fact of one fiscal year at two dates returns both as `Q4`; that is accepted as it stands, since a single item behaves the same. Reported mode is unchanged.
  - Fiscal labels of a date filed under both tags (`_labels`): the plain figure's, as #427 set (where the single item before #421 placed the date), unless the lease-inclusive figure places the date and the plain one does not, or the lease-inclusive one places it as a fiscal year end (`FY`) and the plain one under a quarter. Then the lease-inclusive figure's labels. Two placed labels that differ only in fiscal year (`FY` 2019 against `FY` 2020) keep the plain figure's, as #427 set. Otherwise a placed year end would be dropped from the annual read only because of which tag carried the label: on `main`, a plain year end mislabelled `Q3` was dropped by the filter and the lease-inclusive figure was read alone at `FY`; pairing first would have dropped the date entirely. The labels follow the dates, whichever figure is chosen.
  - `READING_RULES_VERSION` 10 → 11 (the version on `main` before this branch is 10) with its row in `reading_rules.md`: on some data this changes which figure a run reads, though no filing does today.
  - Tests (`tests/analysis/test_current_lease_debt.py`, on the `make_company` fixture edited by `lease_inclusive()` to file Exxon's layout: non-current debt only under a lease-inclusive tag, the current portion under both tags, plain 20 and lease-inclusive 24 dollars): with the lease-inclusive year ends relabelled `Q3` or unplaced, annual and quarterly reads in both modes give the same (date, fiscal year, fiscal period, value) list as before the relabel, every value 24 (each of the eight failed on `main`); the same with the plain year ends relabelled (on `main` these pass except the annual `Q3` case; pairing first with #427's label rule alone would have dropped those year ends, which the new rule prevents); both placed, neither `FY` (plain `Q4` 2019, lease-inclusive `Q1` 2020): the plain labels win, and a chosen plain figure is returned unchanged; through `get_facts` (issue item 2), a lone lease-inclusive 30 at 2019-12-28 under `FY` 2019 gives one fact per label in annual and quarterly mode, both modes, the 2019-12-31 one (24) winning, and 2019-12-28 kept in reported mode (one per date).
  - Exit: full suite and ruff clean.

- **Step 2. A run note when the rounding margin chooses the figure.**
  - `Fact` gains `rounded_plain: float | None` (default `None`): set by `current_on_noncurrent_basis` to the plain figure when the lease-inclusive figure is taken *below* it, through `_within_rounding`. Nothing is stored: facts are read at query time.
  - `math_engine._balance`: the balance sheet's current portion (`history.latest_before("debt_current", balance_date)`, a fact `get_facts` merged) is checked after the #415 and #408 checks, so the note fires only on a figure the run counts. When it carries `rounded_plain`, run note `debt_current_within_rounding` with params `period_end`, `debt_current` (the figure read), `debt_current_plain` (the rounded figure beside it), `rounding_unit` (the plain figure's, as `_within_rounding` uses it), `rounding_margin` (half of it), `gap` (plain minus the figure read), `currency`, `accession_no` (the figure read's filing), `assumptions` (`debt`, `cost_of_debt`, `nwc_pct`), and an English text. It can only fire where the margin can: a lease-inclusive non-current debt at that date. The code joins `RUN_NOTE_CODES`; its lesson in `text.WARNING_LESSONS` (group `data`) says in lay words why a rounded figure and a more precise one can both be right, that Warren reads the precise one, that the filing does not say how much a figure was rounded so Warren guesses it from the trailing zeros, and that a wrong guess understates debt by at most the gap. Its sources: XBRL 2.1, section 4.6, and *Precision, Decimals and Units 1.0* for the range reading; the unit inferred from trailing zeros is Warren's own (professor's blocker). The note changes no figure, so the reading rules row stays under version 11.
  - Tests: the note on the fixture's balance sheet with every plain current portion a rounded 200,000,000 dollars and the lease-inclusive one 195,000,000 (unit 10^8, margin 50M, gap 5M), with its params; no note when the lease-inclusive figure is 210M (above), 200M (equal) or 140M (outside the margin); `rounded_plain` set only when the margin decides; the lessons test covers the new code.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the plain figure's labels always (#427's rule).** Pairing before placing would then drop a year end from the annual read whenever only the plain figure is mislabelled, where `main` read the lease-inclusive figure there. Preferring a placed label, and a year end's over a quarter's, never drops a date either figure placed.
- **Place first and pair across labels** (match a dropped figure to a kept one at the same date). That re-reads facts the placed read threw away: it is the reported read with extra steps.
- **Refuse when the labels differ.** A refusal stops a run over a labelling difference that changes no figure (as #427 argued).
- **A note on every choice of the lease-inclusive figure.** That fires on every Exxon date since 2019 and teaches nothing new: #421's choice is the rule. Only the rounding margin is a judgement call.
- **Raise the floor to 10^8 now** (issue item 4). No filing trips the margin at 10^7 today; the note makes the first one visible, and the floor is revisited the first time the note fires on a plain figure rounded to exactly 10^7.

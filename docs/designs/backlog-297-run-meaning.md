# Backlog #297 item 1 (run part): a run whose revenue crosses a tag's change of meaning

Issue: https://github.com/sonderbot-ar/warren/issues/297 · Branch `backlog-297-run-meaning` · Status: in progress (nov.11.2026; design reviewed, built).

Built here: item 1's second half, the run note. v0.9.49 built its first half (`facts.meaning_state` says "mixed" for a derived quarter worked out across the change); v0.9.50 built item 2 for a series of reported periods. Left in #297: item 2 for trailing sums, track records and forecast errors; items 4, 5, 7, 11, 12, 13.

## Problem

Exxon's `us-gaap:Revenues` meant sales until the 10-Q of 2019-11-06 and total revenues and other income from then on (`facts.MeaningChange`). The reported view says so on a line (v0.9.49) and across a series (v0.9.50). A run says nothing.

A run reads revenue in three places (`math_engine.build_inputs`):

1. **The seed** (`_revenue_seed`): the last four reported quarters, which the forecast grows from; when a year's quarters do not add up to the year, also that year's annual figure (year × each quarter's share).
2. **The quarterly margins** (`_drivers`): EBIT ÷ revenue for each of the last 12 quarters, averaged by fiscal quarter, for forecast quarters 1–8.
3. **The annual history** (`_history_rows`, `_drivers`): the last three fiscal years' revenue, the denominator of the annual margin (years 3–5) and of the D&A, capex and working-capital shares.

Growth (build plan D2, the growth rule) is not one of them: it averages same-filing 10-K comparisons, and each comparison is current year against prior year as one filing shows them, so both sides of each carry one meaning. That is not the whole story (Limits).

On a run dated after the change, these figures can carry different meanings. Example, as of 2020-03-01 (the 10-K of 2020-02-26 refiled 2017 and 2018 on the new meaning): the seed quarters are FY2019Q1 and Q2 (sales, never refiled) and FY2019Q3 and Q4 (total revenues and other income). FY2019's quarters add up to 261,359 against the year's 264,938 (−1.4%), so the year × share rule applies: the seed's total is the year as filed, on the new meaning, and only its split between quarters comes from quarters on both meanings (analyst; seed rows 62,490 / 68,415 / 65,940 / 68,093). The margin window FY2017Q1–FY2019Q4 divides EBIT by sales in 8 quarters and by the broader total in 4, and the annual margin for years 3–5 divides by the broader total. Exxon's EBIT is the fallback (pretax income plus interest), which already includes equity-affiliate and other income, so the old-meaning quarters divide a broad numerator by sales alone (analyst). The issue counted 90 of Exxon's 100 stored runs dated after the change; the analyst's scan found 27 that read figures on both sides (2019-11-15 to 2021-08-01; Step 2).

No number is wrong in the sense of a bug: each figure is what Exxon filed. But the run mixes two definitions without saying so, and the brief's Q3 and Q5 (a run's value and its assumptions) cannot explain an Exxon run honestly.

## Plan

**Step 0. This doc.** No build plan change (backlog item).

**Step 1. The note (`math_engine`).**

- While building, the run records each revenue fact it reads, by part and period label: `seed` (the four seed quarters; when the year × share rule applies to a year, that year's annual figure and all four of its quarters, since they set the shares), `operating_margin` (the margin window's quarters that have revenue, up to 12), `history` (the three annual years). A label in two parts is the same fact (both read `load_history`), so it has one state. Kept on `EngineResult` in fields that are never stored (`revenue_read`, `seed_years_times_share`), so stored runs, rows and snapshots are unchanged.
- `build_inputs` also reads `facts.tag_meaning_changes(conn, cik, ["revenue"], as_of)` and the filing date of every accession of the company (one query, as `reported._Filings` does; the helper moves to `facts.filing_dates` and `reported` uses it), inside the block that already records data warnings. Both are point in time: the changes come from filings public on `as_of`; the filing dates are only used for the accessions of facts already read as of `as_of`.
- Each fact's state for a change follows `reported._series_meaning_notes` (v0.9.50), now shared as `facts.state_for_change`, with `facts.dating_changes` and `facts.mixes_meanings`: `meaning_state` (v0.9.49: "new" when the fact's tag is the changed one and its newest input was filed on or after the change; "mixed" for a derived quarter whose inputs were filed on both sides; else "old"); "old" when under the changed tag before the change or under the other tag; "other" for any other tag (nothing is known of its meaning). The filing-date map is `facts.filing_dates` (all of the company's accessions; only those of facts already read as of `as_of` are looked up).
- Note `run_mixes_meanings`, one per change, ordered by filing date, when across all three parts any figure is "mixed", or at least one is "new" and at least one is not. Old and other with no new figure gives none (nothing on the new meaning is read). Same rule as the series note, so the two never disagree on the same facts. Params: `item` (`revenue`), `tag`, `other_tag`, `changed_on` (ISO, the filing date of the first filing that shows the change, as on every `tag_changed_meaning` note), `form`, `accession_no` (that filing), `old_periods`, `new_periods`, `mixed_periods`, `other_tag_periods` (period labels in time order, quarters `FYyyyyQn` and years `FYyyyy`, a year after its Q4; each label in exactly one list), `parts` (the run parts, in the order `seed`, `operating_margin`, `history`, whose own figures mix by the same rule; empty when each part is on one meaning but the parts differ, e.g. margins on sales applied to a seed on the broader total), `seed_split_years` (the seed years where the year × share rule applied and the year's figures mix: the seed's total for that year is the year as filed, one figure, and only its split between quarters mixes; analyst blocker) and `largest_gap` (the largest relative gap between the two meanings on the periods the change shows, `MeaningChange.periods`; Exxon 3.9%, analyst).
- English fallback, Exxon as of 2020-03-01 (real output): "This run reads revenue as us-gaap:Revenues with more than one meaning: FY2017Q1, FY2017Q2, FY2017Q3, FY2017Q4, FY2018Q1, FY2018Q2, FY2019Q1 and FY2019Q2 carry the tag's earlier meaning; FY2017, FY2018Q3, FY2018Q4, FY2018, FY2019Q3, FY2019Q4 and FY2019 the meaning the 10-Q filed 2019-11-06 gave it. These are worked out on revenue that is not like for like: the revenue the forecast starts from and the quarterly operating margins. For FY2019 the forecast starts from the year's total as filed, split by its quarters' shares, so only the split between quarters mixes the two. On the periods the change shows, the two meanings differ by up to 3.9%; there the earlier meaning is still filed, under us-gaap:RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax. This says nothing about other changes within either side." The `history` part reads "the annual margin and the D&A, capex and working-capital shares" (analyst idea 4). With `parts` empty the second sentence is "Each part of the run is on one meaning, but not the same one, so the margins and shares may not match the revenue the forecast starts from." "Seed" is not used in the text (professor).
- No value, row, method text or source changes. The code joins `RUN_NOTE_CODES`.
- Tests (`tests/analysis/test_run_meaning.py`, Test Corp with `Revenues` 4% higher from its Q3 2019 10-Q filed 2019-10-30 and the old figures kept under the contract-revenue tag, the `_redefine_revenues` edit of v0.9.46, imported from `test_reported.py`): as of 2020-06-30 the note has the tags, date and form, `parts` seed and operating margin, FY2019Q2 and FY2017Q2 old, the 10-K's three years new, FY2018Q4 and FY2019Q4 mixed (Test Corp files no nine months, so its Q4 is the year less three quarters filed on both sides), each label once, `seed_split_years` [2019] (its quarters miss the year), a 4.0% gap, and the text; the same run with no change found has identical rows, sources and other notes (the note changes nothing else); none as of 2019-10-29; none for unchanged Test Corp. The code is in `RUN_NOTE_CODES`. `tests/analysis/test_reported_series.py` passes unchanged (the shared helpers change nothing there).

## Alternatives considered

- **One note per part.** Three notes on most affected runs, saying the same break three times; the `parts` param carries the same information.
- **Note only when a part mixes internally.** Misses the case where each part is on one meaning but they differ (a margin on sales applied to a seed on the broader total), which is the mismatch that matters most for the forecast.
- **Restate onto one meaning.** Item 4 and item 11 (like-for-like growth, margins on sales) ask a version of this. It changes numbers and is a method choice; this step only says where the break is.
- **Include growth.** Each growth figure is one filing's current against prior year, on one meaning, so each is like for like on its own. But the three can sit on different meanings, and growth of the broader total (which includes equity-affiliate and other income) is a different, more volatile series than growth of sales; growth measured on one meaning and applied to a seed on another is the same kind of mismatch as the margin's (professor). Left out here because the comparisons are not `Fact`s the run reads by period; a known gap, said in Limits.

## Product impact

- **Added:** run-note code `run_mixes_meanings`, params as in Step 1, on `run_notes(conn, run_id)` and on a new run's notes. Nothing renamed or removed; no value changes.
- Serves brief Q3 and Q5 (a run's value, its assumptions and its forecast): an Exxon run dated 2019-11-15 to 2021-08-01 can now say its revenue mixes two definitions. `parts` tells the *Fuente* tab which assumptions to mark: `seed` on the revenue path (Q5), `operating_margin` and `history` on the margins and on the D&A, capex and working-capital shares (Q3) (product lead; principles, rule 2). Q1, Q2 and Q6: no change.
- **Where it ranks (product lead).** Like a series note, not like a figure's note: one note above the estimate's value and forecast, read before any single figure's notes, and repeated in the side panel only on the assumptions `parts` names (brief §3, "Notes on a series"). Group 1 of "Notes on a reported figure" ("can't be read as asked") would wrongly tell a student not to read the value per share.
- **Stored runs (product lead gap 1, not taken here).** All stored runs already have their notes recorded, so none of the 27 gets this note until it is made again; stored notes are kept byte for byte equal to `runs.notes`, the text the run printed when it was made (backfill rule, v0.9.15), and adding a note afterwards breaks that pairing. Filed as a backlog idea (a separate "added later" note list with its date). Until then the app can call `reported_series` over the run's periods for the same break.
- Suggested Spanish (product lead and professor), generic as the series note's (no line names until item 5), dates as "6 nov 2019", "etiqueta XBRL" glossed once as in brief Q1 group 2: "Esta estimación usa ingresos de {empresa} con una etiqueta XBRL que no mide lo mismo en todos los periodos: {old} usa(n) la definición anterior y {new} la definición nueva, desde que el {form} presentado el {fecha} cambió lo que mide la etiqueta." Then, when `parts` is not empty: "No son comparables de un lado al otro del cambio los ingresos sobre los que se calcula(n): {partes}." with "los ingresos de los que parte el pronóstico", "los márgenes operativos trimestrales", "el margen anual y las proporciones de D&A, capex y capital de trabajo". When `parts` is empty: "Cada parte de la estimación usa una sola definición, pero no la misma, así que los márgenes pueden no corresponder a los ingresos de los que parte el pronóstico." For `seed_split_years`: "En {año}, el pronóstico parte del total del año tal como se informó; solo su reparto entre trimestres mezcla las dos definiciones." For `mixed_periods`: "{mixed} mezcla(n) las dos definiciones." For `other_tag_periods`: "{other} usa(n) otra etiqueta, de definición desconocida." Never "reexpresado" or "presentación".

## Limits

- Only revenue, and only a change the filings show (`tag_meaning_changes`); a reclassification with no other tag (item 13) is not seen.
- Growth is not checked: its three same-filing comparisons can each sit on a different meaning, and growth on one meaning applied to a seed on the other is not flagged (Alternatives; professor).
- Q5's range (p10–p90) comes from past forecast misses, which may be measured against actuals on the old meaning; that is #297 item 2 (forecast errors), still open (product lead).
- No restating: the note says where the break is, it does not put the run on one meaning (#297 items 4 and 11). Flagging is the interim step; restating onto one definition is the textbook ideal (Koller, Goedhart and Wessels, *Valuation*, 7th ed.; professor).
- EBIT, D&A, capex and working capital are not scanned for a change of meaning; only revenue is (as in v0.9.46).
- Stored runs are not re-noted; the note appears on runs made from now on (Product impact).
